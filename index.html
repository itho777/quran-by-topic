💡 Format Guide

  • All templates have 6,236 rows — one per Quran verse (row 1 = verse 1 = Al-Fatiha 1).
  • Translations: columns verse_id, source_id, text. Source: id.kemenag or en.sahih.
  • Tafsirs: columns verse_id, tafsir_id, text. Tafsir: id.jalalayn or en.katsir.
  • Asbabun Nuzul: columns verse_id, source, text. Source: en.wahidi.
  • Wrap fields containing commas or newlines in double-quotes.
